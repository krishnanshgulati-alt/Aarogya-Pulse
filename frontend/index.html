Menstrual health & anemia

♡

What is anemia?

Anemia occurs when the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin. Iron deficiency is one possible cause.

✦

Know your symptoms

Persistent tiredness, dizziness, weakness or breathlessness deserve attention. These symptoms can have many causes.

＋

When to seek help

Very heavy or prolonged periods, or symptoms that worry you, should be discussed with a qualified health professional. Severe breathlessness, chest pain, fainting or heavy bleeding with marked weakness need urgent care.

❋

Food and testing

Beans, lentils, chickpeas, leafy greens and iron-fortified foods can contribute iron. A clinician may recommend a blood count and ferritin testing when appropriate.

Privacy: This local demo does not ask for your name, age or period dates. Before collecting personal health data from real users—especially minors—add appropriate consent, access controls and a clear privacy policy.
